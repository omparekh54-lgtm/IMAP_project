04 / Recorded benchmark

Where the real
difference showed.

CRUD throughput was broadly similar. The dramatic gap appeared when one DRF sync worker was forced to wait on concurrent I/O.

DRFFastAPI

Once DRF gets enough threads, the huge gap nearly disappears. That shows the first result is mostly about concurrency configuration, not a universal 19× framework advantage.
